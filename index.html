SPACE WEATHER FORECAST

An immersive 4D spatial exploration tool that challenges perception of geometry and dimension through interactive visualization.

TAP THE COUNTRY

An interactive geography learning app where users explore world nations through intuitive map-based gameplay and spatial reasoning challenges.

BOTTLE DRIFT WORLD

Send a message into the ocean and watch it drift around the world. Discover bottles from other users near you in real time.

JUST WALK THE GLOBE

Turn daily steps into a global journey. Walk the Earth virtually as your real-world movement maps to destinations around the planet.

URBAN GREENUP

Mapping and tracking urban greenery health across cities. Empowering communities to monitor and improve their environmental footprint.

AIR & WATER INTELLIGENCE MAP

A next-generation cognitive mapping tool that visualizes how people mentally perceive and navigate urban environments and neighborhoods.

MILK CHECKER

ORBITFIX: GNSS SATELLITE MAP
